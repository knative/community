## Knative Community Calendar

Knative has a shared [calendar](https://calendar.google.com/calendar/u/0/embed?src=knative.team_9q83bg07qs5b9rrslp5jor4l6s@group.calendar.google.com)
where all the events related to Knative are. This includes Working Group, Community, etc. meetings.

Access to add new events to the calendar is available to Maintainers, and will be
migrating to LFX soon.

If there are other members of the community that need to manage the Knative
team calendar on ongoing basis, please reach out to the Maintainers and
we can share the calendar with your
existing account (at least in some cases).

If there's a one-off calendar invite needed, please send a message on
[the #knative channel in CNCF Slack](https://cloud-native.slack.com/archives/C04LGHDR9K7).
